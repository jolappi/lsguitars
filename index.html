05

Design detail

The flame strap guide

A small part that changes how the guitar hangs on you.

Flame strap guideUnique solution

Body dive vs. neck dive

Leans in, not away.

The flame on the back of the guitar is not just decoration — the strap runs through it. That changes the angle the strap pulls from, so the guitar turns toward the player instead of tilting away. The same idea works on round bodies too: with two strap buttons on the body, or with the strap running straight through the body itself.

03 / Origin

Born from
hands & wood.

Lapland Shaman Guitars began in Nokia, Finland, with a simple idea: build distinctive instruments slowly, carefully, and by hand.

In a small workshop in Nokia, traditional hand skills and modern playability meet in every build. The name may look north, but the sawdust, tools, and guitars belong here.

Each guitar is made one at a time—from selecting the wood to the final setup. The result is not a copy of something familiar, but a personal instrument with its own weight, grain, and voice.
